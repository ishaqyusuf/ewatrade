01 / The foundation

Three facts. Three different meanings.

1

“I have paid”

The customer makes a claim. A report is saved for the business to check. The Order is not paid merely because this button was pressed.

2

“Receipt checked”

The attachment appears to match some expected details. AI and rules can help inspect it. They cannot establish that the business received funds.

3

“Confirmed by business”

An authorized person checks the actual bank record and confirms the amount. A successful saved financial transaction then updates the Order and money history.

02 / The whole journey

From unpaid Order to a saved receipt.

Example: a service Order totals ₦50,000. The business asks for a ₦20,000 installment. The customer transfers ₦20,000.

If the money is not found

Choose Not received and give a customer-facing reason. No payment is posted; the customer can check the transfer or contact the business.

→

If only part arrived

Confirm the actual amount under the allowed review policy. Preserve the original claim; show the remaining due rather than falsely marking the whole Order paid.

03 / Learn by changing the amounts

Follow every naira.

The new allocation design follows two different rules: a fresh transfer adds money; existing customer credit spends money the business already holds. The examples assume an eligible linked customer, open accounting period, valid permissions and completed review.

04 / The feature guide

Everything, A to Z.

Base / local source describes the earlier manual-payment implementation. MB-01 / in progress describes the selected extension being built. Future describes deferred automated banking. Labels are not deployment evidence.

A

Accounts & bank instructions

Base / local source

The business uses an existing Nigerian bank account. Finance Owner/Admin records its bank name, ten-digit account number and account-holder name on an eligible BANK money account. The account must allow the relevant payment method and be available for new payments.

These are merchant-entered instructions. Saving them does not verify account ownership with a bank, generate a virtual account, connect bank feeds or move money. Finance setup, account eligibility and plan checks still apply.

B

Business, Store & permission boundaries

Base / local source

Every link, report and payment belongs to one business and an authorized Order scope. A team member’s current Store, Order and creator access is checked again when they review or act; possessing a report ID does not grant access.

Finance management belongs to Owner/Admin. Eligible Order operators can create payment requests and review ordinary receipts within their scope. Excess funds, full customer balances and refund administration require the stronger Finance authority. A forwarded customer link never grants merchant powers.

C

Create a payment link

Base / local source

Start with an eligible unpaid Commercial Order, including a service Order. Select the receiving bank account, requested amount, whether smaller payments are allowed, and a lifetime from 10 minutes to 72 hours. The link freezes the bank instructions and requested amount for that request.

Creating a link does not mark the Order paid. The page shows the Order’s safe item/service details and current financial status. Short /p/… links coexist with older signed links; both are private, purpose-scoped access paths.

Order due ₦50,000; link requests ₦20,000. “Requested” describes the installment. “Order due” describes the whole remaining debt.
D

Different amounts mean different things

Keep these four numbers separate: requested is what the business asked for; claimed is what the customer says they sent; extracted is what a receipt reader sees; confirmed is what the merchant verifies in the receiving account.

Only confirmed funds can be recorded. A mismatch invites review, not silent replacement of the customer’s claim. Requested amount is not automatically the allocation cap when an explicit, allowed larger installment is reviewed.

Requested ₦20,000 · claimed ₦30,000 · receipt appears to say ₦30,000 · bank shows ₦25,000. The financial review must use the verified ₦25,000 and preserve the other facts.
E

Evidence: receipts & attachments

Base / local source

A customer may attach a private JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF receipt up to 4 MiB when attachment entitlement allows it. The server checks actual file bytes, type, length and digest. A receipt is optional: an attachment-free report can still be manually reviewed.

Receipt access is private and tied to the exact report and current authorized merchant scope. A public preview must not expose a private receipt URL. An unreadable or rejected attachment can be replaced; it must not become fabricated payment evidence.

F

Findings from receipt checks

MB-01 / in progress

A purpose-specific reader extracts amount, currency, date, recipient, narration and bank reference when visible. Deterministic rules compare them with the saved request. Missing fields stay unknown. An unreadable, old, wrong-account or amount-mismatched receipt is flagged for review.

“Receipt appears consistent” is advisory. There is no reliable screenshot-only guarantee against forgery or a reversed transfer. If analysis is slow, disabled or unavailable, the customer can submit for manual review. Provider evaluation and approved customer-data admission remain separate gates.

A receipt says ₦30,000, but the customer typed ₦3,000. Offer “Use receipt amount” or “Keep my amount and ask the business to review”. Do not silently change it.
G

Generated payment references

MB-01 / in progress

A readable reference such as EWA-7K4P9D2Q helps match a request to bank narration. The customer copies it into their bank app’s description. The same saved attempt keeps its reference through refresh and sharing; a later installment has its own saved attempt and history.

The reference is a matching hint. It is separate from the Order number, secret link code and bank transaction/session ID. A missing or truncated narration needs a bank check; it is not automatic rejection. Different actual transfers may use the same narration.

H

Human confirmation is the money gate

Base / local source

In Payments to check, the reviewer opens the report, checks the receiving bank, and reviews actual amount, receiving account, receipt date, bank reference and relevant notes. Confirm received records the payment and decision together. Not received requires a reason and records no payment.

The saved review must still match the Order and report version. If another person changes the balance, customer, account or permissions, refresh and review again. A failed save cannot legitimately show a successful payment result.

I

Installments & partial payments

Base / local source

A confirmed partial receipt reduces the Order’s balance and leaves the rest due. Fixed requests retain their amount policy; editable requests permit the allowed partial amount. A second payment is a distinct attempt, not a rewrite of the first receipt.

MB-01 / in progress Larger-than-requested transfers require explicit apply-more review. For a ₦50,000 balance and ₦20,000 request, a permitted ₦30,000 allocation leaves ₦20,000 due. It creates no excess until confirmed funds exceed what is applied.

J

Journal, effective date & history

The canonical Order and Finance commands own accounting. A bank receipt increases recorded money; Order settlement and customer-held funds keep their proper source links. A collection is not automatically new sales revenue: billed and unbilled Orders have different existing posting rules.

The receipt’s effective date records when the money was received. Audit timestamps record when the system saved and approved it. Closed periods and the Book start date still restrict posting. Corrections keep history through controlled reversing/replacement entries rather than deleting receipts.

Do not assume every screen groups dates the same way. Assistant “Orders created today” uses the business timezone; existing Finance report day grouping is UTC. Check the date labels.
K

Keep excess as credit with this business

MB-01 / in progress

When verified money exceeds the chosen Order allocation, the remainder stays held for the actual customer. It is credit with this business, in this currency, backed by a canonical customer-ledger source. Multiple receiving banks do not multiply it.

Credit is not a transferable EwaTrade wallet or merchant profit. A saved customer identity and eligible source are required before spending. For a guest or unresolved customer, authorized resolution must establish ownership; the system must not invent a customer to make the numbers fit.

L

Ledger balances: owed, held, reserved & available

MB-01 / in progress

The customer may owe money on Orders while also having eligible held funds. Keep debt and spendable credit distinct. Finance management can inspect the statement, allocations, source entries and holds. Balance changes come from audited commands; there is no unrestricted balance-edit box.

Available means eligible, unreversed, unallocated funds after active reservations. A known zero balance hides the actionable customer credit row. Loading, unavailable, unlinked and unauthorized are separate states; none should display an invented zero.

₦30,000 unused eligible funds − ₦5,000 refund hold = ₦25,000 available. The reserved ₦5,000 cannot also pay another Order.
M

Merchant notifications & Payments to check

Base / local source

Submitted reports feed the scoped in-app payment inbox. Opening a notice rereads current authority and the saved report; the notice itself is not proof of funds.

Email/WhatsApp alert configuration has local source but live delivery remains gated by plan policy, verified recipients, business settings, capacity and provider activation. Each recipient delivery uses one unit per business, channel and UTC month. The entire selected recipient group is admitted together or remains in-app; unknown send outcomes must be reconciled before retry.

Unavailable policy does not mean unlimited. Exhausted quota does not silently switch channels. Customer-facing Copy/WhatsApp actions are separately reviewed manual sharing, not proof of automated delivery.

N

New Order: use existing credit

MB-01 / in progress

After customer verification, show eligible positive credit for this same business and currency. The customer explicitly chooses Apply credit and reviews the amount, remaining credit and remaining transfer. Opening a link never spends it automatically.

The server rechecks current funds, holds, Order eligibility and consent, then applies exact sources once. Credit-only payment says “Paid with customer credit”. A mixed payment says “Credit applied ₦10,000; transfer ₦15,000”. No new bank receipt is created by the credit application.

For a ₦20,000 installment on a ₦50,000 Order, ₦10,000 applied to that installment leaves ₦10,000 requested transfer and ₦40,000 Order due. Installment remaining and Order due are different numbers.
O

Overpayments: one receipt, two destinations

MB-01 / in progress

A confirmed receipt is conserved: received = applied to Order + held excess. If ₦60,000 arrives for an Order with ₦50,000 due, apply ₦50,000 and hold ₦10,000 for the customer. A refund hold belongs inside that held amount; it is not another ₦10,000.

The composed save must either record every required part or roll everything back. It must not record a full ₦60,000 receipt and then create two extra cash receipts of ₦50,000 and ₦10,000. If the actual amount or allocation differs from the proposal, review again.

P

Proof of the actual customer

MB-01 / in progress

Anyone may forward a payment link. Possession alone cannot reveal a customer’s credit, spend it, request control of a refund destination, or expose their statement. Credit access requires a current authenticated customer identity matched to the exact business Customer, with the relevant purpose and Order checks.

The implementation is wiring the existing login/verified-contact seam; exact end-to-end acceptance remains open. Contact text typed into a public form is not verification. Social previews, unauthenticated HTML and shared messages must carry no private balances or identity proof.

Q

Queues, retries & reopening the page

Base / local source

A stable operation identity lets a repeated submission or confirmation recover its saved result instead of creating another payment. If the page loses network, reread status before trying again. With browser storage unavailable, the page must explain recovery limitations rather than promise durable local recovery.

MB-01 / in progress Receipt checks use durable jobs and attachment generations. Replacing an attachment invalidates old findings. A late AI result can add evidence; it cannot change an already confirmed payment or silently alter the allocation.

R

Refunds: request, reserve, return, record

MB-01 / in progress

A refund request is an intention. It is not an outgoing bank transfer. After source and authority checks, eligible funds are reserved so they cannot also be spent. Finance Owner/Admin reviews identity and destination; destination changes need fresh review.

  1. Request / details
  2. Approve / reserve
  3. Return in bank
  4. Record completion

In the manual design, the merchant returns money through their actual bank, then records confirmed completion with reference/evidence and date. Cancellation or permitted decline releases the hold; the underlying customer liability remains. For an excess claim, the preference may exist before funds are confirmed; it does not create spendable credit or a completed refund.

Eligible unused credit, excess and paid-Order refunds retain their source rules. If an Order was paid partly with credit and partly by bank, the return must distinguish restored credit from actual cash refund so the same value is never returned twice.

S

Services & assistant requests

Base / local source

The same payment-link mechanism supports product and service Orders. Completed service work can still have an unpaid active Order. An assistant can find scoped candidates for a named Order, service or Orders created today, then propose links for one to five exact Orders.

Ambiguous matches must be resolved. Review binds Orders, due balances, account instructions, amount policy and expiry. Explicit confirmation rechecks the entire batch; stale Orders require another review. Replaying the same confirmed proposal recovers its original result.

Bank setup and manual receipt approval remain authenticated forms. New credit/refund writes remain form-only in the MB-01 scope. Assistant or WhatsApp source support does not establish a channel’s live rollout.

T

Transfer identity & duplicate protection

MB-01 / in progress

A file digest identifies identical receipt bytes. A bank transaction identity identifies the actual transfer. A customer payment reference merely helps matching. They solve different problems.

A validated, business-scoped strong transfer identity may be consumed only once across supported report and clerk-entry paths. Two competing confirmations must produce one financial receipt. A legitimate split is multiple allocations of that one receipt. Unverified public drafts must not permanently block transfer identities.

Cropped images, edited screenshots and missing historical references limit image-only detection. Historical matching coverage and all entry paths still require acceptance; no promise is made to catch every duplicate receipt image.

U

Updates, live status & reconnect

Base / local source

The customer outcome comes from the saved report and current Order balance: checking, partially paid, paid or not received. A status socket sends a small “updated” hint; the page rereads authorized server state. A bounded refetch fallback supports checking while sockets are unavailable.

A socket connection is not itself a payment fact. Production needs an explicitly deployed socket-capable runtime; the normal Vercel HTTP deployment alone cannot supply that host. An offline page must not invent a paid outcome.

W

WhatsApp, copying & customer messages

Base / local source

Copy creates text to share. A WhatsApp action prepares a draft for review and manual Send; it does not establish delivery. Outcome drafts use the saved Order contact where available, rather than trusting arbitrary payer details for messaging.

No-phone message-editor work is included in the current implementation wave and remains subject to acceptance. Selecting a recipient for a manual message never verifies customer identity for credit or refunds. Automatic merchant alerts use the separate consent, plan, recipient and provider controls described in M.

X

eXpiry, cancellation & exception recovery

Base / local source

An expired/closed link stops new use through that request. It does not cancel a real bank transfer, undo a confirmed receipt or erase the Order. If money arrived after the link closed, the merchant needs a separate authorized resolution path using the actual transfer facts.

A stale review, revoked permission, changed receiving account or already-paid Order must refuse an unsafe confirmation. Refresh and review; do not bypass the guard. If an accounting period is closed, use the authorized accounting correction workflow.

MB-01 / in progress Applied credit stays on the Order even if the page closes. Order cancellation/amendment uses canonical release/refund rules. An incoming reversal affecting spent credit needs controlled correction, never a silent debit to an unrelated balance.

Y

Your end-of-day checks & reconciliation

Compare the actual bank statement with canonical receipts and the relevant Finance account activity. Check that confirmed totals, Order settlements, held customer funds, refund reservations and completed returns agree. Keep unreviewed reports distinct from real bank inflows.

The existing Bank statement/matching tools are a related Finance workflow; a manual receipt is not automatically bank-reconciled. One bank inflow split between Order and excess should still resolve to one parent transfer, with no second collection. Later provider matching must link to the manual receipt instead of posting it again.

  • Review pending or rejected reports whose transfers need resolving.
  • Explain partial Orders, unused credit and aging refund holds.
  • Investigate statement differences and unknown delivery/dispatch outcomes.
  • Correct with history intact; do not delete entries to force totals to agree.
Z

Zero hidden automation & what comes later

Future

The manual scope does not provision new bank accounts, initiate customer transfers, automatically approve screenshots or pay refunds through a provider. Saving a request, receipt, confirmation or refund record must not be mistaken for physically moving funds.

Future bank/provider adapters can add authenticated reconciliation and approved payout initiation, while preserving the same receipt, allocation, refund and duplicate identities. Unknown payout outcomes require reconciliation before retry. Disabling AI should leave a truthful manual-review path; it must not lose reports or undo committed money history.

05 / Check your understanding

Questions that usually cause confusion.

Does “I have paid” mark the Order paid?

No. It saves an unverified report. Only a successful canonical confirmation of actual received money, or an authorized existing-credit allocation, changes settlement.

Can a receipt be checked and the money still be missing?

Yes. Receipt checks describe evidence, not settlement. The business must inspect its receiving bank account.

Can I confirm a smaller amount than the customer claimed?

The actual bank amount is the financial fact. Review the discrepancy under the current policy, retain the customer’s original claim, and show the resulting balance. If an allocation/preference changed, obtain the required fresh review.

Is excess customer money additional sales income?

No. It is held for the customer until an authorized allocation or return. The canonical Finance source rules determine revenue; the payment event alone does not.

Does a refund request send money back?

No. It captures intent and, after checks, reserves the eligible funds. In the manual workflow the merchant returns the money in their bank, then records confirmed completion.

Can two tabs spend the same credit?

They must not. Server checks, exact-source holds, financial locks and idempotent commands arbitrate competing spends/refunds. The MB-01 concurrency acceptance must prove this before enabling that experience.

Does an expired link make an actual transfer disappear?

No. Link access and money are separate. The business must resolve an actual late transfer through an authorized path; it must not force a stale confirmation through the closed link.

What if the customer sends the old amount after applying credit?

Record the actual bank receipt once. Apply only the eligible current Order amount and resolve the remainder through the reviewed excess/credit/refund path. Do not count the earlier credit application as a second bank receipt.
