## Complete prioritized refinements

1. **Brand color and section rhythm.** White/grey/ink repeat across most of the
   page, leaving WhatsApp as the only vivid moment. v16 explores a warm EwaTrade
   action palette and distinct surfaces while retaining the current layout.
2. **Demo credibility.** Remove QA suffixes, FCA test references, manual-payment
   test labels and internal language such as “immutable order record” from
   public-facing examples. Keep accurate product UI and a discreet “Example
   business” disclosure. Use curated illustrative records, never fake endorsements.
3. **Typography roles.** Serif hero/How it works/features/closing coexist with
   heavy sans WhatsApp/workspace/pricing/FAQ headings. Define one consistent
   heading hierarchy; keep the WhatsApp emphasis intentional. Avoid changing
   fonts simply for novelty.
4. **Hero emphasis and mobile line breaks.** At desktop size little of the
   product is visible in the first screen. At390px the headline occupies four
   lines and leaves “paid.” alone. Refine measure, breaks and vertical spacing
   after the color decision; keep the centered composition and same section order.
5. **WhatsApp repetition and height.** The headline, subheading, paragraph and
   three steps repeat message → draft → approval. Keep the strong announcement
   and readable phone, tighten surrounding copy and surplus spacing. Preserve
   explicit review-before-save semantics and real availability boundaries.
6. **Feature copy and proof.** Eight equally weighted icons make coverage clear
   but labels such as “Units, availability and history” still read as a module
   inventory. Keep the grid and links; sharpen each caption to a concrete owner
   outcome and make each jump reveal the relevant example. Reports correctly
   selected its matching walkthrough state in the reviewed runtime.
7. **Product-preview presentation.** Curate a coherent business/order across
   Orders, WhatsApp, stock and reports. Emphasize the useful figures and readable
   state labels within truthful screens. Avoid dense internal chrome becoming
   the main visual story; retain the explicit native/offline Coming soon status.
8. **Pricing and CTA expectations.** Runtime “Choose Starter/Growth/Pro” leads to
   Contact. Prefer wording that makes the assisted activation clear near the
   action. Explain what the assistant allowance means using the actual plan
   contract, without inventing limits. Retain visible free-plan limits.
9. **Navigation polish.** Runtime Features anchor put its heading near40px under
   the roughly76px sticky header, visibly clipping it. Fix scroll offsets and
   verify header navigation/feature deep links at the affected viewport. This
   should be handled as a focused implementation fix after design review.
