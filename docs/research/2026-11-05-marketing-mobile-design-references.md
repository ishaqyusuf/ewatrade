# Landing page and mobile-feature references

Visually reviewed on 5 November 2026 at the official sites. These are references
for composition, scale, narrative and motion. Their media implementations are
mixed; no claim is made that their product illustrations are pure HTML.

| Reference | Useful lesson for EwaTrade |
| --- | --- |
| [Midday](https://midday.ai/) | Owner-selected centered serif hero, compact animated walkthrough, feature overview, moving rows and large footer wordmark. |
| [Copilot Money](https://www.copilot.money/) | One dominant phone; readable transaction/action details and purposeful state changes. |
| [Shopify Mobile](https://www.shopify.com/mobile) | Merchant sequence: order, detail, fulfilment; analytics gets its own view. |
| [Monzo](https://monzo.com/) | Upright phone proportions, clear states and restrained surrounding composition. |
| [Linear Mobile](https://linear.app/mobile) | Specific actions and product details with concise supporting annotations. |
| [Things](https://culturedcode.com/things/) | Consistent device proportions and quiet interface presentation. |
| [YNAB](https://www.ynab.com/) | Meaningful balances and rows demonstrate value more directly than decorative UI. |
| [Revolut](https://www.revolut.com/) | Selective emphasis on a few amounts/actions instead of every feature at once. |

The owner-selected composition follows Midday, supported by Shopify's merchant
journey, Copilot's product detail and Monzo's upright phone states. EwaTrade retains its own typography, components,
records and approval model. No competitor asset is copied into the design.

The WhatsApp motion shows request → typing → whole reply, ending before approval.
It does not stream characters. A static exchange supports reduced motion, and
Workshop-only controls provide replay/pause for design review.

Midday was inspected live after the owner supplied the reference. EwaTrade adapts
its moving integration rows into business categories drawn from the implemented
marketing source, and its footer into EwaTrade's existing product and policy links.
No integration, compliance certification or customer proof is imported.
