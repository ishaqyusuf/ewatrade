AI SAFETY

OCR helps with typing. It does not
make the clinical decision.
Human checks are built into the operating flow.

1. MACHINE DRAFT

OCR
The system reads the image and produces editable text with confidence indicators.

2. MANDATORY VERIFICATION

HUMAN CHECK
The attendant checks the generated result line by line against the original prescription.

3. PROFESSIONAL RELEASE

PHARMACIST
A licensed pharmacist handles ambiguity, alternatives, restrictions, approval, and dispensing controls.

No raw OCR output becomes a customer quote.
