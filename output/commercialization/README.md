# EwaTrade Prescription Commerce Commercialization Package

This folder contains the editable strategy source. Final presentation files are
stored under `output/pdf/`, and the formula-driven commercial model is stored
under `outputs/ewatrade-prescription-commerce-commercialization/`.

## Intended use

- `output/pdf/ewatrade-prescription-commerce-commercialization-strategy.pdf`:
  internal EwaTrade decision-making.
- `output/pdf/ewatrade-prescription-commerce-pharmacy-partnership.pdf`: send to
  owners and executives after confirming legal and commercial terms.
- `output/pdf/ewatrade-prescription-commerce-mobile-pitch.pdf`: share through
  WhatsApp or review on a phone.
- `outputs/ewatrade-prescription-commerce-commercialization/ewatrade-prescription-commerce-commercial-model.xlsx`:
  replace blue assumptions with pilot or pharmacy data.

## Status

Commercial strategy for validation. Prices, financial results, and KPI targets
are working hypotheses until measured with a pharmacy partner.
