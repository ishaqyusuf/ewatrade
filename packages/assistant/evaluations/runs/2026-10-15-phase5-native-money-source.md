## Implemented source

Three native editors now support existing simple money-in, expense and recorded
transfer actions. Amount first, current account/category choices, paid/unpaid
expense, explicitly staged bookkeeping accounts, optional notes/payee and original
photo removal use native controls. Save updates the ordinary proposal and requires
refreshed review and explicit confirmation. It never posts or sends money itself.

Exact conversion refuses excess precision/unsafe/nonpositive money. The helper
preserves the saved action, Book and exact posting instant; dates are changed in
chat, avoiding a guessed device/business timezone conversion. Only original sent
photo IDs can remain. An unpaid expense removes funding fields; same-account or
ambiguous staged transfers refuse. Generic income remains visibly Owner funds;
loans, deposits, refunds and supplier movements keep their own workflows.

Each editor gets current protected choices through a mount-owned fresh read.
Pending/cached/paused/denied data is hidden and Save disabled. Only the active
action's query is enabled. Current Owner/Admin access and scope remount the editor;
old or absent expense/transfer Books cannot be silently substituted. These are
consumer guards; canonical server authority and signed review remain decisive.

Finance movement and bill receipts now navigate with their saved Book and exact
original ID. The existing routes forward the expected Book to the protected
workspace gate, which refuses another Book. Receipt-origin movement/bill reads
also require fresh mount-owned success and matching record/Book before showing
facts or actions. Ordinary unpinned Finance routes retain their existing behavior.
No fallback to an Order, another Book or an arbitrary current record was added.

Exactly three manifest client lists add mobile; source rollout, Owner/Admin policy,
Book scope, canonical procedures and versions are unchanged. Broader planned
