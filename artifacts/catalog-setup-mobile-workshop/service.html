01 · PRICING

Know the amount, or quote the job.

Fixed price is a known charge before ordering. Quote each job confirms the amount after a request. The accepted quote sets the price.

02 · CUSTOMER CHOICES

Explain the choice before collecting it.

Packages, garment types or repair types create separate offerings. Each can use a fixed price or require a quote. Prices are never copied between choices.

03 · WORK

Track work only when it helps.

Charge only keeps the Service on the Order. Track work creates work after order confirmation and reveals when it may start: confirmation, required payment, or manager release.

04 · SHARED DETAILS

Images and categories have a visible home.

Category → optional Subcategory reuses the shared config. Images describe the service; private job evidence and promised dates belong to the later intake/work flow.
